Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Delete your data

Gustra (developer: Philip Zvar) stores your reviews, photos, settings, and backups on your device. There is no Gustra cloud account.

How to delete your data:

  • In the app — delete individual reviews or visits (swipe / options). That removes those reviews and associated local photos from Gustra.
  • Uninstall Gustra — removes the app’s local data from that device (reviews, criteria, settings, on-device photos managed by the app).
  • Email request — write to philip.zvar@gmail.com with subject “Gustra data deletion” if you need help confirming what to remove (e.g. backups you saved outside the app).

What is deleted: on-device Gustra data for that install (reviews, ratings, notes, app-managed photos, settings). What may remain: copies you exported yourself (backups, shared .gustrashare files, photos you saved to your gallery), and data briefly processed by Google Places/Maps or Gemini when you used those features (subject to those providers’ policies). We do not keep a separate Gustra server copy of your reviews.

No additional retention period applies on our side beyond what stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall.

1. Who we are

Gustra (“the app”) is a restaurant review app by Philip Zvar. Contact: philip.zvar@gmail.com · Website: gustra.net

2. What Gustra stores on your device

Your reviews, ratings, criteria, photos, wine notes, settings, and backups are stored locally on your device. Gustra does not create a cloud account and does not require you to sign in.

3. Permissions we ask for

  • Camera — take photos for your profile and restaurant reviews (including wine labels).
  • Photos / media — import images for reviews and profile; optionally save review photos to your library when you choose.
  • Location (while using the app) — find nearby restaurants. Location is used for search and maps; it is not sold or used for advertising.

4. Data sent to third-party services

Only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Google Places / Maps — restaurant search, place details, and map display. Queries and map usage are processed by Google under Google’s terms.
  • Google Gemini (Vision) — optional wine-label scan. The cropped label image (and related prompt) is sent to Google’s Gemini API to identify wine details. Do not scan labels you do not want processed this way.
  • Sharing you start — when you share a review (file, email, or system share sheet), content goes to the apps/services you choose.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show ads in the app.

5. Backups and imports

Encrypted backups and shared review packages stay under your control (Files / chosen storage). Importing a friend’s share stores their review data locally on your device.

6. Children

Gustra is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your choices

  • Deny camera, photo, or location permission in system settings (some features will not work).
  • Delete reviews or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Avoid wine-label scan if you do not want label images sent to Gemini.

8. Crash diagnostics

To find and fix crashes, Gustra may send technical diagnostics (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and recent navigation steps) to Sentry. This does not include your review text, photos, or location by default. You can limit this by not installing tester builds that include crash reporting, or by contacting us to opt out of future diagnostic builds.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: philip.zvar@gmail.com
