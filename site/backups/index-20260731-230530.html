Features

Every meal, remembered

Gustra helps you keep honest notes on the places you eat — then finds them again when it matters.

Reviews that feel like yours

Open any visit for full scores, photos, and notes — like “Delicious pasta!” — across Food, Drinks, Service, Atmosphere, and Value for Money.

Your map of memories

See every place you’ve reviewed on a personal map. Spot clusters of favorites and rediscover meals from trips you almost forgot.

My Gustra

Track how your tastes evolve — visit counts, average scores, and your best restaurants gathered in one elegant overview.

How it works

Three steps to a living food diary

Simple enough for after-dinner notes. Rich enough to become your long-term culinary archive.

1

Find the place

Search nearby restaurants or pick from the map, then start a review in moments.

2

Capture the meal

Rate what mattered, add photos, and note down the details you want to remember later.

3

Return anytime

Browse your feed, explore your map, or open your passport when you’re choosing where to go next.
