Features

Every meal, remembered — your way

Gustra is a personal journal, not a feed of other people's opinions. It keeps honest notes on the places you eat, then helps you find them again — and share them — when it matters.

Memories that feel like yours

Score a meal on Food, Drinks, Winery, Service, Atmosphere and Value for Money. Add photos, keep private notes, and save drafts to finish later. Because these are memories, not reviews to publish to the world.

Scan a wine label, keep the story

Point your camera at an etiket and Gustra builds a wine card — estate, grape, vintage, region, serving temperature and food pairings. Your bottle becomes part of the memory, ready to revisit and rate.

Your map of memories

See every place you've visited on a personal map. Spot clusters of favourites, rediscover meals from trips you almost forgot, and head straight back with built-in maps & navigation.

My Gustra

Track how your tastes evolve — visit counts, average scores, your best restaurants and top cities, gathered in one elegant overview.

Share across iPhone & Android

Gustra works everywhere your phones do. Send a friend your whole food diary as a Gustra package — restaurants, memories and photos — and they import it with one tap, on iPhone or Android.
